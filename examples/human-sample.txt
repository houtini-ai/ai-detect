I've been using the ProGrind X1 for about three months now. The grind's consistent enough that my pour-overs finally taste the way I wanted, and I don't have to fiddle with the dial every morning. It's not silent, though. First thing I noticed unboxing it was how heavy the burrs feel compared to my old blade grinder. Would I buy it again? Probably, yeah. It's a bit pricey, but I haven't had to think about grind size since, which is the whole point.
